Ibuprofen Concentration and Degradation Analysis

Chemistry lab project, January to May 2026

Measured how UV and heat exposure affect ibuprofen content in commercial tablets

  • Found ibuprofen concentration in tablets by acid-base titration with NaOH (1:1 neutralization).
  • Built a 4-point calibration curve in Python with matplotlib relating NaOH volume to concentration.
  • Ran 10+ titrations across UV-exposed, heat-exposed, and control groups.

Identifying an Unknown Weak Acid

Formal lab report, written in LaTeX

Identified the unknown as mandelic acid from its molar mass and pKa

  • Standardized NaOH against potassium hydrogen phthalate to 0.1065 ± 0.0011 M.
  • Located equivalence points with the derivative method and Gran plots; trials agreed within 0.5%.
  • Calculated a molar mass of 147 ± 8 g/mol and a pKa of 3.50 ± 0.06.
